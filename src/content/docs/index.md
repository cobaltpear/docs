Cobalt Pear is a web page: you type a prompt and get a picture from your own endpoint.
The app is at [cobaltpear.github.io](https://cobaltpear.github.io/), and the setup steps are in the [guide](https://cobaltpear.github.io/guide.html).

This manual explains what the buttons do and which choice fits you.

- [Choosing a model](models/) — *Add*, *Custom*, or your own worker
- [LoRAs](loras/) — adding LoRAs, and choosing between *Built into the worker* and *By link*
- [News](news/) — what changed, newest first
